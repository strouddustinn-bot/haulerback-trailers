# Haul 'Er Back Trailers — Business Plan (public draft)

Ottawa enclosed cargo and utility trailers. Basic · Pro · Wrap tiers across Snowbird, Trailhead, Jobsite, and Roadhaul, plus the Jobsite 612 Lite prototype.

> Public draft. Per-unit costs, prices, margins, and rent-based break-even are summarized on the [pricing page](pricing.html). Investor names, capital sources, and funding-based payback figures are intentionally omitted from this public document.

## Overview

- **Business:** Design and manufacture of enclosed utility trailers in Ottawa, Ontario
- **Customers:** Contractors, landscapers, snowmobile and ATV owners, recreational and equipment-transport buyers
- **First prototype:** Jobsite 612 Lite (lightweight single-axle 6×12 with shelving, plan desk, cooler seat)
- **Vision:** A recognizable trailer brand with a repeatable production line, Ontario first

## Product tiers

| Tier | What it is |
|------|------------|
| Basic | Affordable everyday utility — standard build |
| Pro | Upgraded finishes, galvanized/powder frame options, lighting, E-track, HD where it fits |
| Wrap | Pro plus full custom wrap (customer brand or Haul 'Er Back design) |

## Customer financing

Buyers can finance or lease trailers through **third-party** lenders once Haul 'Er Back is set up as a dealer merchant. This is customer credit at point of sale — not company funding.

### Prerequisites

Ontario business registration, Class A garage licence, business bank account and void cheque, ID, sample invoice, and an NSM / compliance plan.

### Recommended stack

| Partner | Role |
|---------|------|
| [Financeit](https://www.financeit.io/frequently-asked-questions/) | Primary for regular retail buyers — easy signup, no monthly/transaction fee on the standard program, funded in ~2 business days; promo buy-downs cost a % of the purchase |
| [Prefera Finance](https://preferafinance.com/dealers/) | Towable trailers — terms up to 15 years; published rates from just over 10% to ~28% by credit tier; prefers established dealers |
| [LendCare](https://www.lendcare.ca/en/businesses/recreational-vehicle-financing) | Weaker-credit buyers — any business can apply; approval in a few days; dealer reserve |
| [National Bank Equipment Finance](https://www.nbc.ca/en/business/financing/equipment-finance/dealers.html) | Contractor lease-to-own (quote-based); used by Stittsville Trailer in Ottawa |
| [LeaseLine](https://leaseline.com/why-lease/) | Contractor lease-to-own (quote-based); also used by Stittsville Trailer |
| [Canada Powersports Financing](https://www.canadapowersportsfinancing.com/) | Ottawa broker backup (818 Boyd Ave) with 20+ lenders |

Local reference: [Stittsville Trailer finance](https://www.stittsvilletrailer.com/finance/).

### Later / check-fit

- [National Bank RV & trailer retail](https://www.nbc.ca/en/business/my-business/manufacturing/retail-industry.html) — from ~$7,500, up to 240 months
- [Desjardins leisure vehicle loan](https://www.desjardins.com/en/loans-credit/auto-other-vehicle-financing/recreational-vehicle-loan.html) — up to 20 years
- [Meridian OneCap](https://www.meridianonecap.ca/leasing-financing) — commercial / vendor leasing
- Check first: [iFinance](https://ifinancecanada.com/offer-financing/) ($500–$40k, from 9.99%, no merchant fee), [Flexiti](https://www.flexiti.com/for-partners), [Alterna Outdoor Adventure Loan](https://www.alterna.ca/en/personal/borrowing/loans)

### Poor fits

Scotia Dealer Advantage, BMO Dealer Finance, Rifco, Snap, Fairstone, Northlake (site offline).
