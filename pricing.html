Rent-based break-even (trailers / month)

Fixed costs = rent + about $1,500/mo for insurance, utilities, phone, software and marketing (no owner salary). Blended margin assumed $2,500/unit early on (Lite mix ~50% Basic / 35% Pro / 15% Wrap).
