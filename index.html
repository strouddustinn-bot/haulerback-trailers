FAQ

Where are you built?

Ottawa, Ontario — local design and manufacture of enclosed utility trailers.

When can I buy?

Join the waitlist for launch updates. We are not promising a hard delivery date on this page.

Do you ship?

We are starting with local and Eastern Ontario pickup. Wider delivery can be discussed once production is rolling.

Brakes and weight?

Snowbird 712 and the Jobsite 612 Lite are aimed under Ontario's 1,360 kg brake threshold (2,990 lb GVWR). Tandem models typically need electric brakes and a breakaway kit. Details on request and on the models page.

Can I wrap my brand?

Yes — the Wrap tier is a Pro build plus a full custom wrap.

What about licensing to manufacture?

We keep a public Licensing Checklist with ordered steps, costs, timelines, and source links for Ontario / federal requirements.
