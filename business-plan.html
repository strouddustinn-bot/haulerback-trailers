Haul 'Er Back Trailers — Business Plan (public draft)

Ottawa enclosed cargo and utility trailers. Basic · Pro · Wrap tiers across Snowbird, Trailhead, Jobsite, and Roadhaul, plus the Jobsite 612 Lite prototype.

Public draft. Per-unit costs, prices, margins, and rent-based break-even are summarized on the pricing page. Investor names, capital sources, and funding-based payback figures are intentionally omitted from this public document.

Overview

  • Business: Design and manufacture of enclosed utility trailers in Ottawa, Ontario
  • Customers: Contractors, landscapers, snowmobile and ATV owners, recreational and equipment-transport buyers
  • First prototype: Jobsite 612 Lite (lightweight single-axle 6×12 with shelving, plan desk, cooler seat)
  • Vision: A recognizable trailer brand with a repeatable production line, Ontario first

Product tiers

Manufacturing strategy

  1. 1. Finalize the Jobsite 612 Lite design
  2. 2. Lock a bill of materials with real quotes; set tier prices
  3. 3. Build the prototype, jigs, and weigh/road-test
  4. 4. Document cut lists, weld sequence, QC, VIN/label log
  5. 5. Replicate from the same jigs; expand the range as demand shows up

Marketing and sales

  • Social build videos and handovers
  • Site with specs, tier pricing, waitlist (home)
  • Kijiji / Facebook Marketplace / AutoTrader
  • Direct outreach to contractors with Wrap as the hook
  • Local shows and dealer consignment

Jobsite 612 Lite — first prototype cost (steel)

First build is steel. Aluminum welding gear is deferred and is not part of the first trailer's cost.

LineEstimate (CAD)
Materials + parts~$4,500
Labour$1,400–$2,800
Overhead (shop consumables, small tools, freight on parts, etc.)$500–$1,000
Total first-build cost~$6,400–$8,300
Target selling price at ~30% profit margin (price = cost ÷ 0.70)~$9,100–$11,900

Deferred — not in first trailer cost: aluminum welding gear ($4,000–$8,000) is a later shop purchase. Do not fold it into prototype materials, unit cost, or the target selling price.

Customer financing

Buyers can finance or lease trailers through third-party lenders once Haul 'Er Back is set up as a dealer merchant. This is customer credit at point of sale — not company funding.

Prerequisites

Ontario business registration, Class A garage licence, business bank account and void cheque, ID, sample invoice, and an NSM / compliance plan.

Recommended stack

PartnerRole
FinanceitPrimary for regular retail buyers — easy signup, no monthly/transaction fee on the standard program, funded in ~2 business days; promo buy-downs cost a % of the purchase
Prefera FinanceTowable trailers — terms up to 15 years; published rates from just over 10% to ~28% by credit tier; prefers established dealers
LendCareWeaker-credit buyers — any business can apply; approval in a few days; dealer reserve
National Bank Equipment FinanceContractor lease-to-own (quote-based); used by Stittsville Trailer in Ottawa
LeaseLineContractor lease-to-own (quote-based); also used by Stittsville Trailer
Canada Powersports FinancingOttawa broker backup (818 Boyd Ave) with 20+ lenders

Local reference: Stittsville Trailer finance.

Later / check-fit

  • National Bank RV & trailer retail — from ~$7,500, up to 240 months
  • Desjardins leisure vehicle loan — up to 20 years
  • Meridian OneCap — commercial / vendor leasing
  • Check first: iFinance ($500–$40k, from 9.99%, no merchant fee), Flexiti, Alterna Outdoor Adventure Loan

Poor fits

Scotia Dealer Advantage, BMO Dealer Finance, Rifco, Snap, Fairstone, Northlake (site offline).
