Customer financing

Third-party lenders and brokers that can finance buyers of Haul 'Er Back trailers. This is not company capital — it is consumer / commercial credit offered at checkout once we are set up as a dealer merchant.

What this page is: a recommended stack for Ottawa enclosed-trailer sales (retail buyers, weaker-credit buyers, and contractor lease-to-own). Exact dealer fees and minimum years-in-business were mostly unpublished — confirm with each lender when you apply.

Prerequisites before you apply

  • Ontario business registration (sole prop name or corporation)
  • Class A garage licence (trailers)
  • Business bank account + void cheque
  • Government ID
  • Sample invoice and an NSM / compliance plan (lenders want to see you can legally sell completed units)

Recommended stack

PartnerBest forWhy it fitsNotes
Financeit Regular retail buyers Easiest merchant signup; no monthly or transaction fee on the standard program; paid in about 2 business days Promo rate buy-downs cost a % of the purchase — budget that as a sales expense
Prefera Finance Towable trailers Terms up to 15 years; published rates from just over 10% to about 28% by credit tier Prefers established dealers — apply once the shop and Class A licence are live
LendCare Weaker-credit buyers Any business can apply; approval in a few days; dealer reserve structure Useful backstop when prime lenders decline
National Bank Equipment Finance (formerly CWB National Leasing) Contractor lease-to-own Used by Stittsville Trailer in Ottawa; quote-based rates Strong fit for Jobsite / commercial buyers
LeaseLine Contractor lease-to-own Also used by Stittsville Trailer; quote-based Pair with National Bank Equipment Finance for coverage
Canada Powersports Financing Broker backup (Ottawa) Local broker at 818 Boyd Ave with 20+ lenders Good overflow when direct dealer programs are slow or decline
