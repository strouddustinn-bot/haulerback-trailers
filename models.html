Compliance steps every unit

  1. National Safety Mark

    Transport Canada authorization to build trailers, in place before the first sale.

  2. WMI and VIN

    Manufacturer code from the CVMA; unique 17-character VIN per unit to CMVSS 115.

  3. Build to applicable CMVSS

    Including 108 (lighting), 115 (VIN), 120 (tires/rims); keep a build file per VIN.

  4. Compliance label

    Permanent label on the forward half of the left side with GVWR, GAWR, NSM, VIN, bilingual conformity statement.

  5. NVIS to the buyer

    New Vehicle Information Statement plus bill of sale; buyer registers at ServiceOntario (trailer plate about $72).

  6. Sell under Class A garage licence

    Keep the 2-year purchase and sale record. See the Licensing Checklist.

Style basis

  • Diamond-plate stone guard, matching fenders
  • Rear ramp with spring assist; curb-side man door with RV lock
  • All-LED exterior lighting; Haul 'Er Back decal on nose and sides
